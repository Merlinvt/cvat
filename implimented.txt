  How It Works:

  1. User selects a skeleton label and chooses "3 Clicks" drawing method
  2. Click 1: Sets top-left corner of bounding box
  3. Click 2: Sets bottom-right corner (box becomes visible)
  4. Click 3: Places keypoint inside/near the box
  5. Annotation is saved as a skeleton with a single keypoint element
  6. Tool automatically resets for the next annotation
